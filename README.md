 This repository houses a collection of dynamic and insightful visualizations created using Power BI and Tableau. Dive into the world of data analysis with interactive dashboards covering weather analysis, Amazon and Netflix show trends, and AI-driven retail sales. Explore, discover, and gain valuable insights to inform decision-making and drive business success. 

### 1. **Mental Health Report (Tableau Dashboard)**:
   - **Treemap**: Displays a proportional breakdown of responses based on changes in mental health. Categories include "Yes", "No", and "Maybe", providing a high-level view of the population’s sentiment.
   - **World Map**: Visualizes mental health data geographically, enabling users to analyze mental health trends and history across different countries.
   - **Line Graph**: Tracks the decrease in mental health issues over time by occupation and country, revealing key patterns for different professions.

### 2. **Netflix and Amazon Prime Video Comparison (Power BI Dashboard)**:
   - **Pie Chart**: Compares the content availability between Netflix and Amazon Prime Video, highlighting the proportion of content available on each platform.
   - **Gauge Chart**: Tracks user ratings on IMDb, displaying the sum of movies or shows released over the years across both platforms.
   - **Line Chart**: Maps the relationship between IMDb scores and content availability, visualizing how content quality varies between Netflix and Amazon Prime.

### 3. **Retail Sales and Profit Analysis (Power BI Dashboard)**:
   - **Bar Chart**: Compares sales and profit trends by year and region, offering insights into how performance varies across time and geographies.
   - **Line Graph**: Shows month-over-month sales by region, helping to identify seasonal patterns and regional sales fluctuations.
   - **Scatter Plot**: Displays the relationship between the quantity sold and the profit, allowing for the identification of overstock or underperforming products by region.

### 4. **Weather Data Analysis (Tableau/Power BI Dashboard)**:
- **Location Details**: A table lists various global locations, showcasing real-time temperature and humidity values, enabling users to analyze environmental data by region.
- **Temperature Analysis (Line Chart)**: Visualizes temperature trends across different countries, offering a comprehensive view of regional temperature variations and highlighting potential climate anomalies.
- **Humidity Analysis (Pie Chart)**: Breaks down humidity distribution globally, giving users an easy-to-understand snapshot of humidity patterns and their frequency across regions.
